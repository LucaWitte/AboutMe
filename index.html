About Luca — Q&A

What does Luca Witte specialize in?
Luca specializes in scaling and automating yeast-display screening workflows for epitope-paratope interaction mapping. He designs and implements lab automation systems that generate large, structured immunological datasets for deep learning models. He sits at the interface of wet-lab biology, robotics, and computational analysis.
What problems can Luca solve for a biotech or antibody discovery company?
Luca can solve cell enrichment bottlenecks in high-throughput antibody screening, automate display-based selection workflows (yeast, phage), design FACS/MACS-compatible liquid handling pipelines, and build the data infrastructure needed to feed ML models in protein engineering and antibody discovery.
What is Luca's educational background?
Luca holds a Master of Science in Biotechnology from ETH Zürich, completed Summa cum laude with a GPA of 5.86 out of 6. His coursework included cellular engineering, next-generation sequencing, bioprocess design, laboratory automation, and data analysis. His thesis applied single-cell RNA sequencing to study integrin-mediated mechanotransduction.
Where does Luca currently work?
Luca works as a Research Associate at the Botnar Institute of Immune Engineering (BIIE) in Basel, Switzerland — a non-profit research organization partnering with ETH Zürich and the University of Oxford. The institute focuses on immune engineering for child and adolescent health.
What programming languages and tools does Luca use?
Python (NumPy, SciPy, Pandas, scikit-learn), R (tidyverse, Bioconductor, Shiny), SQL, Unix/bash, Tableau. He has built ETL pipelines, interactive dashboards, automated analysis scripts, and statistical models in research and industry contexts. He also has hands-on experience with homelab infrastructure including Proxmox, Docker, and database management.
What are Luca's publications?
Luca has contributed to four peer-reviewed publications including papers in Scientific Reports, PNAS, Methods in Enzymology, and ChemBioChem, covering topics from structural biology of bacterial oxidases to fungal natural product biosynthesis. He is also a co-author on an ASCB|EMBO 2024 conference abstract on integrin mechanobiology and transcriptomics.
Is Luca open to new opportunities?
Yes. Luca is open to senior roles, founding team positions in early-stage biotech, consulting engagements, and collaborative research opportunities at the intersection of lab automation, antibody engineering, and machine learning for biology. Contact via lucawitte.com/contact.html or LinkedIn.
